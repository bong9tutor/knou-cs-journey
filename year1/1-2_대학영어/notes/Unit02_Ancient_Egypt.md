## 본문

### 1

Ancient Egypt was an impressive civilization that arose around 3100 B.C.E. It remained a major power in North Africa and nearby regions until the Macedonians took over in 332 B.C.E. The civilization thrived mostly because it made the most of the Nile River’s flood patterns for farming. The predictable flooding and controlled irrigation of the fertile valley produced surplus crops, which allowed the population to grow and society to become more complex. By successfully managing their environment and resources, the Egyptians established a culture rich in art, knowledge, and architecture that would influence the world for centuries to come.

### 2

In ancient Egypt, the majority of the population was made up of farmers. Their houses were built from mudbrick with designs optimized to provide relief from the intense daytime heat. The staple diet consisted of bread and beer, supplemented with vegetables such as onions and garlic, and fruit such as dates and figs. Hygiene and personal grooming were of significant importance to the ancient Egyptians. Their hygiene system was complex, encompassing bathing, shaving, dental care, and more. Many bathed in the Nile River, employing a homemade paste for soap composed of animal fat and chalk. Body shaving was a common practice and perfumes and scented oils were used to mask unpleasant smells and to calm irritated skin. Music and dance were popular entertainments. Early on, the musical scene was dominated by flutes and harps. As time went on, other instruments resembling trumpets, oboes, and various types of pipes were introduced and gained popularity.

### 3

The architecture of ancient Egypt includes some of the most famous structures in the world: the Great Pyramids of Giza and the temples at Thebes. Building projects were organized and funded by the state not only for religious and commemorative purposes, but also to reinforce the wide-ranging power of the pharaoh. These massive stone structures were believed to safeguard the physical and spiritual essence of the pharaohs. The ka, which is a vital aspect of the pharaoh’s soul, was thought to dwell alongside their mummified forms. The meticulous preservation of the pharaoh’s body was crucial for enabling the departed ruler to adhere to his responsibilities in the afterlife as the monarch of the deceased. In the ethos of Ancient Egypt, death on the earth was the start of a journey to the next world.

### 4

The religion of ancient Egypt was an intricate network of polytheistic doctrines and ceremonial activities, playing a pivotal role within the fabric of its society. The pantheon comprised approximately 1,500 divinities, each commanding reverence and worship. The pharaoh occupied a distinct position, bridging the mundane and the divine, participating in the divine realm, and constructed great, religiously motivated funerary monuments for his afterlife. Pharaohs were obligated to retain the benevolence of the gods through rituals and offerings so that they could maintain Ma’at, the order of the cosmos, and repel Isfet, which was chaos. Notable for their diverse representations, Egyptian deities assumed various forms, including those of animals or composite beings with an animal’s head atop a human frame.

## How to Use Verbs in Sentences

### 1. arise

- If the opportunity arises, I’d love to go to China.
- Problems arise when the payment is late.
- He died of complications arising from heart surgery.

**확인 사항**

- arise 동사의 주어로 opportunity, problems, complications 등이 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 2. remain

- The doctor ordered him to remain in bed for a few days.
- Most commentators expect the basic rate of tax to remain at 25 percent.
- A great many things remain to be done (= have not yet been done).
- He remained silent.
- It remains a secret.
- The bank will remain open while renovations are carried out.
- Only a few hundred of these animals remain today.

**확인 사항**

- remain 동사가 1형식과 2형식 문장에서 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 3. establish

- He helped to establish the University of California at Berkeley.
- Once we establish the price, we can begin to market the product.
- There is a strong need to establish effective communication links between parents, staff, pupils, and external bodies.
- His reputation for carelessness was established long before the latest problems arose.

**확인 사항**

- establish의 목적어로 university, price, communication links, reputation 등의 명사가 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 4. supplement

- He supplements his income by working in a bar at night.
- Some vegetarians like to supplement their diets with iron.
- The new law was introduced to supplement the existing regulations, aiming to close loopholes and enhance enforcement.

**확인 사항**

- supplement의 목적어로 income, diets, regulations 등의 명사가 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 5. calm

- The teacher’s gentle voice managed to calm the excited students within minutes.
- The storm finally began to calm down after raging for hours.

**확인 사항**

- calm 동사가 자동사, 타동사로 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 6. irritate

- Certain skincare products irritated her sensitive skin.
- It irritates me when people are late without giving any notice.
- After a while, the noise began to irritate him.

**확인 사항**

- irritate 동사가 어떤 맥락에서 사용되는지 확인한다.

### 7. dominate

- The cathedral dominates the landscape for miles around.
- The dispute is likely to dominate the news.
- They work as a group — no one person is allowed to dominate.

**확인 사항**

- dominate 동사가 자동사, 타동사로 모두 사용되었음을 확인한다.
- dominate 동사가 어떤 맥락에서 사용되는지 확인한다.

### 8. resemble

- As he grew older, he began to resemble his grandfather, not just in looks but also in his behaviors.

**확인 사항**

- resemble 동사가 타동사로 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 9. introduce

- The company plans to introduce 45 new models over the next five years.
- We need to introduce more stringent security measures such as identity cards.
- I’d like to introduce my son.
- The director will introduce the film personally.
- In the workshop, we were introduced to the basics of digital photography by a professional photographer.

**확인 사항**

- introduce 동사가 사용되는 다양한 맥락을 살핀다.

### 10. organize

- Most British schools organize social events for the students.
- The books were organized on the shelves according to their size.
- I am trying to organize my office so I can work more efficiently.
- They organized a car to pick me up at the airport.

**확인 사항**

- organize의 목적어로 events, books, office, car 등의 명사가 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 11. command

- The officer commanded his men to shoot.
- He commanded that the troops (should) cross the water.
- Colonel Sailing commands the Guards Regiment.
- The old castle commands a stunning view of the surrounding countryside.
- She commands respect from her peers.

**확인 사항**

- command 동사가 to 부정사, that 절과 함께 쓰였음을 확인한다.
- command 동사의 목적어로 regiment, view, respect 등의 명사가 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 12. occupy

- The programme will occupy that half-hour slot before the nine o’clock news.
- All physical objects occupy space.
- Troops quickly occupied the city.
- An increasing number of women occupy senior positions on Wall Street.
- He occupied himself with painting and gardening after his retirement.

**확인 사항**

- occupy 동사의 목적어로 slot, space, city, position 등의 명사가 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 13. bridge

- The new educational program aims to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application.
- The river had been bridged at its narrowest point.

**확인 사항**

- bridge 동사의 목적어로 gap, river 등의 명사가 사용되었음을 확인한다.

### 14. assume

- Let’s assume that they’re coming and make plans on that basis.
- We can’t assume the suspects to be guilty simply because they’ve decided to remain silent.
- Upon her retirement, her successor will assume all responsibilities associated with the position.
- He assumed a look of indifference but I knew how he felt.

**확인 사항**

- assume 동사가 that 절, to 부정사와 함께 쓰였음을 확인한다.
- assume 동사의 목적어로 responsibilities, look 등이 사용되었음을 확인한다.
